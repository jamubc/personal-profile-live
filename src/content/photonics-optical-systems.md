## Overview

Coursework and lab projects in advanced photonics covering fiber optic
coupling, index-matching, Fresnel reflectivity, photodetector
characterisation, and semiconductor bandgap analysis.

## Fiber Optics & Coupling

Analysed single-mode and multi-mode fiber coupling efficiency,
calculating numerical aperture, acceptance angle, and splice loss.
Performed index-matching experiments to minimise Fresnel reflections
at fiber interfaces.

## Photodetector Characterisation

Characterised PIN and avalanche photodiodes, measuring responsivity,
dark current, and noise-equivalent power across the visible and
near-IR spectrum. Compared experimental results against theoretical
models for silicon and InGaAs detectors.

- PIN and APD responsivity measurements across 400–1600 nm
- Dark current and NEP characterisation at multiple bias voltages
- Fresnel reflection calculations for multi-layer dielectric stacks
- Energy bandgap extraction from optical absorption edge data

## Results & Learnings

These labs deepened my understanding of electromagnetic wave
propagation, semiconductor optoelectronics, and precision optical
measurement techniques — skills directly applicable to sensor design
and communications systems.

## Overview

Conducted a comprehensive techno-economic feasibility analysis for a
hydrogen fuel-cell tram-train network spanning 342 km across the
Okanagan region, with an estimated capital cost of $1.5–2.9 billion.

## Economic Modelling

Performed NPV, BCR, and IRR calculations using a 30-year discounted
cash flow model. Revenue projections were based on ridership forecasts
from comparable regional transit systems, with sensitivity analysis
across fare and utilisation scenarios.

- Net Present Value analysis over 30-year project horizon
- Benefit-Cost Ratio evaluation against alternative transit modes
- Internal Rate of Return sensitivity to hydrogen fuel price
- Monte Carlo simulation with 10,000 iterations for cost risk quantification

## Technology Assessment

Evaluated hydrogen fuel cell maturity using Technology Readiness
Levels (TRL), comparing PEM fuel cells against battery-electric
and diesel alternatives. Assessed green hydrogen production viability
from regional hydroelectric surplus.

## Results & Learnings

The study found a positive BCR under optimistic ridership scenarios,
but identified hydrogen production cost as the primary risk factor.
This project developed my skills in large-scale infrastructure analysis,
financial modelling, and technical report writing.

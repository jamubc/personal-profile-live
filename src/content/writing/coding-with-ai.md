Right now? It’s Claude, with Codex quickly catching up.

## Claude

Anthropic’s Claude has made waves with its CLI tool, Claude Code. It’s my go-to for general coding and is especially strong at tool calling (think MCPs).

## Codex

OpenAI’s Codex, which was lagging early on, is now up to speed. It impressed me in recent tests and, after Gemini CLI’s fast entry, the competition heated up. Claude Code isn’t the only game in town anymore.

## Other mentions

We got Gemini CLI, which spawned in forks for other models such as Qwen. I imagine there are many more clones, determined by noticing Gemini CLI's distinct TUI. Grok code, althought at its price point I honestly have only tested within its tight free limits and grok code fast which I suppose is impressive compared to years back, as most of this is anyways, but doesnt impress today.

## Key Takeaways

Choosing between Claude and Codex mostly comes down to **budget and limits**. Both cost money, and both have message caps. Claude’s pricier, always has been. Subscription is the norm, but APIs are available if you want to spend more. Remember: you’re investing time and cash to use these.

Let’s be real: these are probability machines. Your results depend on your inputs. Garbage in, garbage out.

You can also consider using other tools such as opencode, with its ability to connect with github copilot subscriptions, claude subscriptions and openrouter or ollama.

Grok will usually code anything you want including malicious code, sometimes grok wont even dispute these requests which hopefully will be addressed...

## My thoughts on everything else.

Yes, there are all-in-one suites of tools and creature comforts that absolutely deliver and work, and no I have no tried them all and honestly could not if i tried as they have popped up like wildfire, usually with a subscription.

 If you boil it all down, its at a CLI tool no different than Claude, it has some specific instructions, deterministic tools, "context" solutions but at the end of the day it can all be acheivied with any of the CLI tools mentioned here, no need for another subscription or development app.

 You can pay for a subcription that solves your niche, or you can spend hours refining your own system, using a CLI tool at the core. 

 ## Its going to get worse

 The more requests, the more the costs and its felt on both user and company.

 Its no secret these platforms are bleeding money, running inference is expensive. 
 
 Over time since debut Claude rapidly devolved, restructure and "re-push" into a new norm. There was a time when claude was an 'absolute demon'. Overall 'ability' decreased after models were repacked as updates while simultaneously more 'features' were packed in, coming out the other side you would think it was always like this.
 
 Slowly prices increased, limits tightened and users complained. But, I cant blame Anthropic, are they suffering from success or was this the plan all along?
